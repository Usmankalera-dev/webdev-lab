AI Tutor

Ask a question about the course — in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu. The tutor answers from the 23 lessons, and links you to the exact lesson.

How this tutor works

  1. Lessons are split into small pieces. All 23 lessons are cut into short chunks (about 150–250 words each), one chunk per topic.
  2. Your question finds the best pieces. The tutor searches those chunks for the words in your question and picks the 4 most relevant ones. No key is needed for this step.
  3. The AI answers only from those pieces. With your free key saved below, the AI reads just those 4 pieces and writes a short answer from them — with links. If the pieces do not contain the answer, it says so instead of guessing.

Without a key, the tutor still works: it shows you the matching lesson pieces and their links. Your key (if you save one) stays in this browser only.
